Etiquette

To ensure that your time with us, and the experience of our other guests, remains a peaceful retreat, we kindly ask that you review our spa guidelines.

Arrival & Preparation

Arrival Time: Please arrive at least 15 minutes prior to your scheduled treatment to allow time to unwind and change. New guests will also need to complete a brief pre-consultation form.

Gate Access: When you arrive, please give us a missed call or feel free to hoot gently, and we will open the gate for you.

What to Wear: We provide cozy Spa robes in the winter and light Spa wrap-arounds in the summer. Please remember to bring your own flip-flops for your comfort.

Booking Policies

SECURING YOUR BOOKING

A 50% non-refundable deposit is required at least 24 hours before your booking.

CANCELLATIONS

Please cancel or reschedule at least 12 hours before your treatment time.

LATE ARRIVALS

Late arrivals will result in reduced treatment time; the full fee still applies.

The Paradys Environment

Digital Detox: To maintain our tranquil atmosphere, please ensure that all cellular and mobile phones are switched off during your treatments.

Age Preferences: We prefer our clients to be 10 years and older. Guests younger than 16 must be accompanied by an adult for the duration of the treatment.

Right of Admission: Right of admission is reserved. We reserve the right to deny access to any person acting in an inappropriate or unsafe manner.

Health & Safety

Medical Disclaimer: Services are for relaxation and educational purposes only and are not diagnostic or a substitute for medical care.

Minors: For guests under 18, a parent or guardian must sign a consent form prior to treatment authorizing our therapists to administer techniques.

Personal Property: Please store items safely at home or in provided lockers. The spa is not liable for loss or damage to personal property.

LIABILITY

Treatments and facilities are used at the client's own risk. Beauty Unique Outdoor Day Spa will not be held liable for any adverse reactions or injuries suffered on the premises.

PURCHASES

Products purchased at the spa are strictly non-refundable. We appreciate your understanding in maintaining the integrity of our retail offerings.

PRICING

All prices are subject to change without prior notice. Please confirm current rates at the time of your booking to ensure accuracy.
